## D1: Who are the key players in container shipping and what does each do?

**Why asked:** CLT is a logistics company. You must understand the business ecosystem you'll be building software for.

**Answer:**

| Player | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Shipper (Exporter)** | Sends the goods. Prepares cargo, books with carrier or freight forwarder, submits shipping instructions. Named as "shipper" on the Bill of Lading. |
| **Consignee (Importer)** | Receives the goods. Handles import customs clearance and takes delivery. Named as "consignee" on the B/L. |
| **Freight Forwarder** | Intermediary arranging transport on behalf of shippers/consignees. Does NOT own vessels. Handles documentation, customs, cargo insurance, multi-modal coordination. Examples: DHL Global Forwarding, Kuehne+Nagel. |
| **NVOCC** (Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier) | Acts like a carrier to shippers but doesn't own vessels. Books space on actual carriers, issues its own House Bill of Lading (HBL), consolidates LCL cargo. Key distinction from freight forwarder: NVOCC issues its own B/L and accepts carrier liability. |
| **Carrier (Shipping Line)** | Owns or operates the vessel. Issues the Master B/L. Sets sailing schedules and base freight rates. Examples: Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Evergreen. |
| **Customs Broker** | Licensed professional handling customs clearance. Prepares/submits customs declarations, classifies goods under HS codes, calculates duties/taxes. |
| **Terminal Operator** | Manages the port terminal — cranes, yard equipment, gate operations. Examples: PSA International, APM Terminals, DP World. |
| **Trucker / Haulier** | Provides inland transport between port and shipper/consignee premises. Pre-carriage (to port) and on-carriage (from port). |
| **CFS Operator** | Runs the Container Freight Station warehouse. Consolidates LCL cargo into containers (export) or separates them (import). |

**Key distinction to remember:**
- Freight forwarder = agent, acts on behalf of shipper, does NOT issue its own B/L
- NVOCC = acts as carrier, issues its own HBL, accepts liability
