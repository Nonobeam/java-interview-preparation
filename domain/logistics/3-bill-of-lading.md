## D3: What is a Bill of Lading? What are the key types?

**Why asked:** The B/L is the most important document in container shipping. Any logistics system must handle B/L management.

**Answer:**

A **Bill of Lading (B/L)** is the most critical document in shipping. It serves three legal functions:

1. **Evidence of contract of carriage** — proof that carrier agreed to transport the goods
2. **Receipt of goods** — confirms carrier received the described cargo in stated condition
3. **Document of title** — the holder of the original B/L has legal ownership of the goods

### Key B/L Fields
Vessel/Voyage, Port of Loading (POL), Port of Discharge (POD), Shipper, Consignee, Notify Party, Container No., Seal No., Description of Goods, Gross Weight, CBM, Freight (Prepaid or Collect), Date of Issue.

### B/L Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **MBL (Master B/L)** | Issued by the ocean carrier (shipping line). Covers the actual vessel. |
| **HBL (House B/L)** | Issued by freight forwarder or NVOCC to their customer. The carrier only sees the MBL. |
| **Original B/L** | Printed, issued in sets of 3 originals. Must be physically surrendered at destination to release cargo. |
| **Telex Release** | Carrier sends electronic message to destination agent to release cargo without original B/L. Shipper surrenders originals at origin first. |
| **Surrendered B/L** | Original B/L returned to carrier at origin, "SURRENDERED" stamp applied. Cargo released at destination without original. |
| **Sea Waybill** | Non-negotiable. NOT a document of title. Released to named consignee on identity proof. No original needed. Used for trusted trade relationships. |
| **Clean B/L** | No remarks about cargo condition. Required for Letter of Credit transactions. |
| **Claused / Dirty B/L** | Contains remarks about damaged/defective cargo. Banks reject this for L/C. |
| **Straight B/L** | Non-negotiable. Named consignee only — cannot be transferred. |
| **Order B/L** | Negotiable. Made "to order" or "to order of [bank]". Can be transferred by endorsement. Used in L/C transactions. |
| **Through B/L** | Covers multi-modal transport (truck + ocean + rail). One document for the whole journey. |

### Freight: Prepaid vs Collect
- **Prepaid**: Shipper pays the freight at origin before departure.
- **Collect**: Consignee pays the freight at destination before cargo release.

### Why Telex Release Matters
Traditional original B/L takes time to courier to the destination. If the vessel arrives before the document, the consignee cannot pick up cargo. Telex release solves this — increasingly the norm for short-sea routes (e.g., Vietnam to Singapore).
